# Privacy Policy for PublishFlow

**Last updated:** January 2026

## 1. Introduction

PublishFlow (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use PublishFlow.

## 2. Information We Collect

PublishFlow may collect limited information when you connect your LinkedIn account, including:

- Basic profile information (such as name and profile ID)
- Content metadata required to publish or manage posts
- Authentication tokens provided via LinkedIn OAuth

We do **not** collect sensitive personal data.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

- Authenticate users via LinkedIn
- Enable publishing, scheduling, or managing content on LinkedIn
- Improve app functionality and reliability

## 4. LinkedIn API Usage

PublishFlow uses LinkedIn APIs in accordance with **LinkedIn’s Developer Policies**.  
Data accessed from LinkedIn is used **only** for the intended functionality of the app and is not used for unrelated purposes.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- We do not sell, rent, or trade user data
- Access tokens and any stored data are protected using reasonable technical safeguards
- Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** share your data with third parties except where required to operate the service or comply with legal obligations.

## 7. User Control

You may revoke PublishFlow’s access to your LinkedIn account at any time via your LinkedIn account settings.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
